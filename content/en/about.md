# About Me

Hello! I am **Dinar Permadi Yusup**, a **Frontend Developer & Digital Craftsman** based in Indonesia. I dedicate my craft to exploring the intersection between visual artistry (*craftsmanship*) and robust web engineering.

---

## Design & Code Philosophy

1. **Visual Consistency**: Enforcing structured design tokens with 11-shade color ramps and mathematical typography scales based on the *Golden Ratio* ($\phi \approx 1.618$).
2. **User Experience (DX & UX)**: Crafting intuitive, accessible interfaces with delightful micro-interactions.
3. **High Performance**: Optimizing Core Web Vitals, SSR payloads, and edge computing integrations (Cloudflare Pages, D1).

My journey began with a fascination for how digital interfaces shape user emotions and efficiency. Over time, I specialized in the **Vue.js** and **Nuxt** ecosystem for its reactive elegance and solid fullstack architecture.
