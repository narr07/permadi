## Geometry Clicks When You Can Touch It

In a textbook, a cube is a flat drawing. Students count edges and vertices that hide behind dashed lines, and many end up memorising numbers without ever seeing the shape.

**Baruang** turns flat and solid shapes into objects students can rotate, zoom, and tap. They see for themselves where a cube's 12 edges come from.

## Key Features

- **7 3D solids**: cube, cuboid, triangular prism, square pyramid, cylinder, cone, and sphere. Faces, edges, and vertices are numbered so a class can count them together.
- **Unfolding nets**: every solid except the sphere opens and folds back like origami.
- **8 flat shapes** with layers for sides, angles, diagonals, height, and unit squares, plus an animated perimeter.
- **Interactive properties**: tap a property and the matching part of the shape lights up.
- **Step-by-step formulas** for perimeter, area, volume, and surface area that update as you drag the dimensions.
- **4 games and a 10-level quiz**: Quick Guess, Sort the Objects, Little Architect, and Memory Cards, plus a quiz with stars, XP, and player titles. Questions are shuffled every round.

## Engineering Behind It

Baruang is built with **Flutter** and Material 3. A small custom engine drives all the 3D: perspective projection, flat shading, the painter's algorithm, and a hinge tree for the unfolding nets. Every shape is drawn with `CustomPainter` rather than 3D image assets, which keeps the app small and fast.

::tip
Baruang works offline, shows no ads, collects no personal data, and supports dark mode.
::
