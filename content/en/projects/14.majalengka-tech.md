## Local Work Deserves a Local Stage

Developers and designers in Majalengka keep shipping, but their work is scattered across GitHub, Behance, and personal social feeds. Creators in the same town often have no idea who is building what.

**Majalengka Tech** gathers that work in one place. Every creator gets a profile, every project gets its own page, and visitors can leave appreciation and comments.

## What You Can Do Here

- **Project showcase**: browse every published project, filter by category, sort by newest or most popular, and search by title, technology, or creator.
- **Project pages**: a gallery of up to 8 images, the story behind the project, the creator's role, the tech stack, and links to the demo, design, and source code.
- **Creator profiles** at `majalengka.tech/username`, with a downloadable creator card that doubles as a link preview.
- **Creator dashboard**: publish a new project, save drafts, edit your profile, and check how complete it is.
- **Admin pages**: manage users and projects, including Curator's Picks.
- **Content for AI tools** at `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt`.

## Technology

| Part | Technology |
|---|---|
| Framework | Nuxt 4 |
| UI components | Nuxt UI 4 and Tailwind CSS 4 |
| Guides and changelog | Nuxt Content 3 |
| Database and files | NuxtHub with Cloudflare D1 and R2, via Drizzle ORM |
| Sign-in | Better Auth (email, GitHub, Google) |
| Hosting | Cloudflare Workers |

The project is open to contributions. Bug reports, ideas, and pull requests are welcome on the repository.
