# Personal Portfolio Bento Grid Redesign

An in-depth case study on transforming a traditional linear portfolio layout into a dynamic, 12-column modular **Bento Grid** architecture.

## Background & Challenge

Conventional single-column portfolio feeds treat all content equally, diluting focus on flagship projects and technical depth.

## The Solution: Bento Grid Architecture

1. **Weighted Information Hierarchy**: Allocating card footprints (1×1, 2×1, 1×2, 2×2) based on content priority.
2. **Golden Ratio Scale**: Utilizing a $\phi \approx 1.618$ scale to maintain distinct typographic hierarchy.
3. **Floating Island Navigation**: A 3-island segmented navbar with backdrop blur and pill highlights.
4. **Single Spotlight Focus**: Directing attention exclusively to the primary flagship showcase.
