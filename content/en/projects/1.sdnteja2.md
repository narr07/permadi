## Project Overview

The official website for **SDN Teja 2** was designed and developed as a digital school information hub, simplifying access to announcements, event documentation, and educator profiles for parents and the public.

### Key Features

- **School News & Event Center**: Dynamic publishing of school announcements and events.
- **Realtime Database Integration**: Utilizing Supabase for fast and reliable storage.
- **Fast SSR Performance**: Built on Nuxt.js for optimized mobile rendering.
