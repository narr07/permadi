## Streamlining School Financial Accountability Without Data Risks

School treasurers and administrative operators manage complex monthly and semester financial reports based on the official Indonesian Ministry of Education database, **ARKAS**. Compiling general cash ledgers, tax withholding slips, bank reconciliation statements, and official travel documents manually is time-consuming and error-prone.

**[SIBOS](https://sibos.permadi.dev/)** is an open-source desktop solution built on an uncompromising security principle: **Strictly Read-Only**. SIBOS never alters or writes to the original encrypted ARKAS database, guaranteeing 100% data integrity and protection against database corruption.

## Core Capabilities

- **Automatic General Cash & Tax Ledgers**: Instantly generates official Cash Books, Bank Reconciliation Ledgers, and Tax Withholding statements (PPh 21, 22, 23, and PPN) sorted by fiscal periods.
- **Voucher & Receipt Generator**: Produces formatted A2 procurement receipts and official expenditure slips ready for physical signatures and audits.
- **Audit & K7 Managerial Reports**: Generates standardized managerial closure registers for School Principals and School Committees.
- **Integrated School Ecosystem**: Operates seamlessly alongside **[NILUJI](https://narr07.github.io/niluji/)**, modernizing both educational CBT evaluation and administrative governance.
