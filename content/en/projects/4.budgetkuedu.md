## Background & Real-World Need

Tracking daily operational expenses and petty cash in school environments often faces bottlenecks in data recording. Treasurers frequently purchase small supplies on the go, while enterprise desktop accounting systems require formal desktop sessions that delay immediate tracking.

**BudgetKu Edu** was built as a lightweight, self-service mobile bookkeeping tool to capture financial inflows and outflows immediately at the point of transaction.

::note
**Independence Notice**: This application is an independently created tool and is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any governmental entity.
::

## Architecture & Privacy-First Design

Engineered with **Flutter** and **Dart** adopting an *offline-first* architectural pattern:

- **Local Storage Security**: All transaction records remain stored strictly within the local device storage without transmitting sensitive financial records to external cloud databases.
- **Rapid Transaction Entry**: Visual icon tagging and color markers designed for 5-second expense logging.
- **Automated Summary Exports**: Instant generation of standardized bookkeeping summaries formatted for PDF export and spreadsheet analysis.

::tip
The offline-first architecture guarantees zero dependency on network connectivity, ensuring uninterrupted functionality across remote school locations.
::

## Key Features

- **Rapid Expense & Income Logging**: Streamlined entry forms to capture receipts and cash disbursements on the fly.
- **Budget Flow Breakdown**: Visual progress charts to track remaining operational allowances by expense category.
- **Document Exporting**: Generate formatted financial ledger summaries in PDF and tabular spreadsheet formats.
- **Local Backup & Restore**: Simple database export utility enabling manual data transfer to personal cloud storage.
