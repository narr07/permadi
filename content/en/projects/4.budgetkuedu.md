## Project Overview

**BudgetKu Edu** is a Flutter-based mobile app designed to simplify school budget tracking and operational fund record-keeping with structured financial reports.

> **Disclaimer**: This application is independently developed and is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any government agency.

### Key Features

- **Income & Expense Tracking**: Fast categorization of school financial transactions.
- **Financial Report Export**: Export bookkeeping summaries in PDF or spreadsheet format.
- **Secure Local Storage**: Data is safely stored on the user device.
