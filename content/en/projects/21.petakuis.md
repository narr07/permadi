## Questions You Have to Find First

Most classroom quizzes live on a single screen: a question shows up, students tap an answer, done. **[Petakuis](https://petakuis.permadi.dev/)** adds one step before answering: students have to find the question on a map. Each student moves an avatar through a pixel art world, follows a compass to the next question post, and races the class to answer. Correct and fast answers earn the most points.

Students enter the 6-digit PIN from the teacher's screen, pick a nickname and a character, and start playing. No account, no app install. A demo room with PIN **123456** is open any time.

## For Students

- **8 maps to explore**: a coast, a valley, a campus, windmill highlands, a market town, a riverside village, a lakeside village, and a farm village.
- **Controls that fit the device**: keyboard (WASD or arrow keys) on laptops, an on-screen joystick on touch screens, diagonals included.
- **Three question types**: multiple choice, true/false, and short answer.
- **A class leaderboard** that updates live during the game.
- **Accessible mode** to open the next question without walking to the post.

## For Teachers

- **A three-step quiz builder**: pick a map, pick the grade and subject, then write questions, import them from CSV, or draft them with AI.
- **Free or Linear mode**: students find questions in any order, or get guided from the first question to the last.
- **A host screen** for the projector: PIN and QR code, a radar of student positions on the map, and the leaderboard.
- **Score reports** per student and per question, exportable to CSV.
- **Sample quizzes for grades 1–6** built from Indonesia's Kurikulum Merdeka textbooks: Math, Indonesian, Science & Social Studies (IPAS), Civics, Informatics, and English.

## Scoring Happens on the Server

Answer keys never reach the student's device. The game server receives player positions 15 times per second, checks the distance to each question post, grades answers, and keeps the score. One room holds up to 200 students. Rude nicknames are filtered automatically, and there are no ads.

## Free for Schools

Every map, game mode, and report is available without a subscription. Petakuis runs on a self-managed VPS with Docker Compose, which keeps running costs low.

## Technology

A **Bun** monorepo with a **Nuxt 4** + **Nuxt UI v4** + Tailwind CSS 4 + Pinia web app, a realtime **Bun + Socket.IO** game server, and a shared package holding the map catalog and the scoring engine. Quiz data and reports live in SQLite (Drizzle ORM), and teacher sign-in uses Better Auth.
