## Editors Change Pages, Developers Stay Calm

Marketing sites, portfolios, and blogs never stop changing. If every copy or image tweak waits on a developer, the site falls behind. Yet a CMS with no guardrails tends to break the design.

**Sanity × Nuxt UI** sits in between. Editors build pages from pre-designed blocks in **Sanity Studio**, and the **Nuxt** site renders each block with **Nuxt UI** components. The design stays consistent while editors stay in charge of the content.

## Key Features

- **13 page-builder blocks**: hero, features, card grid, timeline, image gallery, rich text, testimonials, FAQ, pricing, call to action, callout, logo cloud, and collection list.
- **Collections without code**: create a Blog, Gallery, Projects, Services, or Team straight from the Studio. Each one gets a list page and entry pages automatically.
- **Visual Editing**: click any text in the Presentation tool to edit it, with a live draft preview.
- **Site search** (`Ctrl/⌘ + K`) across pages, collections, and entries in the language being viewed.
- **Optional multilingual setup**: each page is translated as its own document, and URLs get a language prefix such as `/id/about`.
- **Full SEO** via Nuxt SEO: sitemap, robots.txt, schema.org, and on-theme OG images for pages without one.
- **Swiss-style theme** with scroll-driven animations in pure CSS, in light and dark mode.

## Ready to Use

Spin it up with a single `npm create sanity@latest -- --template narr07/sanity-nuxtui`, then deploy to Vercel, Cloudflare, Node, or as a static site. Sanity TypeGen keeps everything type-safe from schema to component.
