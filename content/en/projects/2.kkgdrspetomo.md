## Project Overview

A collaborative platform for the **KKG dr. Soetomo** educator community, enabling elementary school teachers to share teaching modules, lesson plans, and workshop schedules.

### Key Features

- **Headless CMS with Sanity**: Allows administrators to update content easily with an intuitive UI.
- **Organized Resource Downloads**: Curated repository of lesson plans, syllabi, and assessment tools.
- **Responsive & Clean UI**: Modern design optimized for mobile and desktop devices.
