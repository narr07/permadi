## Empowering Pedagogical Excellence Through Structured Checklists

Amid the demanding schedules of everyday classroom teaching, educators frequently encounter administrative friction that detracts from meaningful teacher-student engagement. Pedagogical standards and instructional best practices outlined across modern curriculum frameworks can often feel abstract or difficult to audit autonomously.

Without an intuitive self-reflection instrument, teachers struggle to pinpoint which areas of their instruction are thriving and which pedagogical elements require intentional refinement, ranging from differentiated planning to formative feedback loops.

**CeklisGuru** was developed as an open-access digital checklist utility, guiding educators to systematically audit, track, and elevate their teaching routines.

## Technical Architecture & Design Principles

Constructed with **Nuxt 4** and **Nuxt UI v4**, CeklisGuru adheres to privacy-first, offline-capable engineering standards:

- **Eight Comprehensive Educational Pillars**: Covering the complete pedagogical continuum: Lesson Planning, Classroom Execution, Assessment & Evaluation, Classroom Management, Professional Growth, EdTech & Innovation, Instructional Models, and Community Engagement.
- **Client-Side Data Persistence**: Checkmarks and completion records store automatically within the browser's reactive `LocalStorage`, operating entirely without user registration, credentials, or remote database synchronization.
- **Visual Progress Feedback**: Dynamic category progress bars and percentage meters provide teachers with instant feedback on their instructional milestones.
- **Mobile-First Responsive Layout**: Clean, accessible typography optimized for smartphones and classroom tablets with minimal data overhead.

::tip
Local storage ensures complete privacy for teacher evaluations while guaranteeing reliable offline functionality in schools facing unstable connectivity.
::

## Key Feature Capabilities

- **Intuitive Category & Sub-Topic Hierarchy**: Structured checklist categories facilitating quick audit sessions during active lesson preparation.
- **Instructional Model Syntax Checklists**: Concrete evaluation checklists designed specifically for Discovery Learning, PBL, PjBL, Inquiry, and Cooperative Learning methodologies.
- **Persistent Offline Readiness**: Operates reliably within mobile browsers without risking progress loss upon closing application tabs.
- **Zero Ads and Open Source**: Distributed as a dedicated educational contribution to empower educators across Indonesia.
