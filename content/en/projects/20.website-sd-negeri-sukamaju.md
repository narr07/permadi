## What Parents Look For on a School Website

Most parents open a school website on their phone, and usually for one task: reading the latest announcement, checking new student enrollment (SPMB) dates and requirements, looking at upcoming events, or contacting the school. This concept arranges every page so those tasks take only a few taps.

SD Negeri Sukamaju 01 is a fictional school. Its name, data, and photos are placeholders.

## An Identity Taken from the School Uniform

The colors come from the elementary school uniform every Indonesian recognizes: red, white, and dark ink on a warm paper-toned background. Red appears only on primary buttons and key markers. All text is set in Plus Jakarta Sans, with thin lines in place of heavy shadows.

## Pages in the Concept

- **Homepage**: the single most important announcement, the latest news, the next four events, an enrollment summary, and a contact card.
- **SPMB (enrollment)**: a stage timeline, the Domisili, Afirmasi, and Mutasi routes explained, a four-step flow, a form whose error messages explain how to fix the input, and an FAQ.
- **Profile, News, Agenda, and Gallery**: official school data, news by category, a monthly calendar, and event photo albums.
- **Mobile version**: a single column, tap targets of at least 44px, and a WhatsApp button that stays within thumb reach.
